The total

There is nothing to type. The bill comes to what each event would be billed on its own, plus any charges you add on its page: one-offs, rentals, and percentage fees or discounts. It is worked out again whenever the events or charges change, until the bill is paid.

Events for different clients

Events booked under different clients can share one bill, such as a term of films booked partly under "LNL" and partly under "LNL + SocComm Films". You are asked to confirm it, and the bill goes to the client under Bill to.
