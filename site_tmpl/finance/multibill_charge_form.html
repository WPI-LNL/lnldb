Kinds of charge

One-off: something the whole bill pays for once, such as an extra crew call. A negative price is a credit.

Rental: gear hired in for the whole run. LNL's rental fee is added on top, as it would be on an event, unless you untick it.

Percentage: a fee or discount on what the events come to, such as 10 for a 10% fee or -10 for a 10% discount. It is rounded down to the cent, as an event's own fees and discounts are.
