How the opening works

The books start on {{ books_start|date:"M j, Y" }}. Whatever each account held at the end of {{ night_before|date:"M j, Y" }} is its opening balance, and every line from then on is counted on top.

Workday only knows the account's total. If some of it was SGA budget money, or SGA still owed LNL a reimbursement for spending before then, say so here. Whatever you do not assign is the account's own money.

Saving replaces the opening split, so this page always shows what the opening is.
