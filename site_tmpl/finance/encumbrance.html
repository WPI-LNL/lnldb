How this works
  • An encumbrance sets money aside against a budget now, before the charge reaches Workday.
  • Give it a line for each fund the purchase is charged to. A line with no description uses the encumbrance's.
  • It stays Pending until its Workday line is imported. That line then offers it in the queue under Already encumbered?. Match it there: settling the line the ordinary way charges the budget twice.
