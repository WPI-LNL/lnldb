How this works

An encumbrance sets money aside against a budget line right now, so the remaining balance you see reflects what has actually been committed — not just what Workday has charged.

Give it a line for each fund the purchase is charged to. A line with no description of its own uses the encumbrance's.

It stays Pending until the matching line shows up in a Workday export. Encumbrances can never be marked Settled on their own.

When that line is imported it appears in the ingestion queue under Already encumbered?, with this encumbrance on the list. Matching it there closes the encumbrance against the real charge — settling the line the ordinary way instead would charge the budget twice.
