How this works

An encumbrance reserves money against a budget line right now, so the remaining balance you see reflects what has actually been committed — not just what has cleared the bank.

It stays Pending until the matching line shows up in a Workday export. Encumbrances can never be marked Settled on their own.

When that line is imported it appears in the ingestion queue under Already encumbered?, with this row on the list. Matching it there closes the encumbrance against the real charge — reconciling the line the ordinary way instead would charge the budget twice.
