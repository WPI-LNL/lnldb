Lines
How lines work
  • Approved: leave it blank while the line is Pending SGA Approval, and enter 0 if SGA Denied it. Neither can be charged to.
  • Refunds charged against a line restore its balance.
  • Categories: + Line adds a line under a category, and Move to moves one to another. Removing a category moves its lines to No category.
  • Request ID: the line's four digits from SGA, which Workday memos quote, e.g. Velcro restock, consumables, (4521).
  • Projection: a line's side locks once anything is charged to it.
